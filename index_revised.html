04

Research projects & contributions

Work spanning the additive manufacturing pipeline
Metal AM · L-PBF

Investigates the influence of process parameters, build plate variability & interruptions on micro-mechanical behavior and property variation in SS316L and Ti6Al4V parts, supporting Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)-aligned qualification and inspection goals. Uses Magics, Autodesk Netfabb, EOS Print and Altair Inspire for support generation and build preparation.

Polymer AM · ULTEM 9085

Aerospace qualification style specimen fabrication via Stratasys FDM, with dimensional inspection, mechanical testing, and NDE to study print/part quality and defect formation.

Materials · Mechanical Testing

End-to-end mechanical and micro-mechanical evaluation using tensile, compression, bending, nanoindentation-based hardness/modulus mapping, SEM, profilometry, and wire-EDM specimen preparation.

AI / ML · Property Classification

Data-driven workflows integrating experimental measurements, statistical analysis, visualization, and machine learning to predict and classify property changes and interruption regions in AM parts.

NDT & in-situ monitoring

Ultrasound, Ultrasound Thermography, Acoustic Emission, connecting nondestructive signals to stiffness and defect-related behavior in support of quality control.

Computational Engineering & Programming

Engineering software, automation, signal processing & data

I use programming to turn experimental and sensor data into repeatable engineering workflows. My coding work supports mechanical testing, NDT, statistical analysis, machine learning, visualization, and manufacturing research.

Python · Mechanical Testing

Automated Mechanical Test Analyzer

Processes stress–strain datasets and supports calculation of elastic modulus, 0.2% offset yield strength, ultimate tensile strength, elongation, regression statistics, and multi-specimen comparisons.

Python · NDT

Ultrasonic Signal & TOF Analysis

Signal-processing workflows for filtering, peak detection, time-of-flight calculations, wave-velocity estimation, visual validation, and automated result export.

Python · Statistics

Manufacturing Data Analysis

Automated ANOVA, Tukey comparisons, regression, parameter correlation, batch plotting, and visualization for additive-manufacturing experiments.

Machine Learning · AM

Process-Interruption Classification

Machine-learning workflow for classifying interruption-affected regions using experimentally measured mechanical-property data from L-PBF components.

Machine Vision · Industry 4.0

Digital Manufacturing & Inspection

Application of image processing, machine vision, sensor-based quality inspection, data visualization, and digital-manufacturing concepts to improve process monitoring and engineering decision-making.
